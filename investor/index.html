Why we exist

VisionA future where the people who make the culture also own it.

Why it mattersTalent is everywhere. Access isn’t. Most creative systems show up only after someone has proven themselves. Idu House is built to show up earlier.

The name

Idu, said EE-doo. A place where things begin.

Origin

Idu is an older historical name for the Edo people and Edo land in present-day Nigeria. In Edo cosmology, the land once called Idu is understood as the origin of the world. Idu House exists to be a place where things begin: where creators find themselves, collaborators meet and stories become films.

Afrofuturism

The house is intentionally rooted in Afrofuturism, building futures from African and diasporic history, identity and invention. That cultural voice is the founders’ own, and it’s the part of the model that can’t be fabricated.

Rooted in where we come from. Built for what we can become.

The problem

The creator economy grew up. The infrastructure serving it didn’t.

Creators

Directors, musicians and animators are building careers, not one-off projects. Self-employment runs 48% among musicians, 65% among writers and 63% among animators. The work gets assembled across projects, relationships and deal structures that rarely run as one system.

The work compounds. The career doesn’t.

Brands

U.S. creator ad spend is expected to reach $44B in 2026. Brands speaking to specific cultural audiences need directors and artists who know that language, and finding the right creators is their top creator-marketing challenge. Measurement and operating tools lag the spend.

The talent exists. The infrastructure doesn’t.

Sources: BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook (self-employment by occupation); IAB 2025 Creator Economy Ad Spend and Strategy Report.

The platform

Threshold, from Idu House.

A threshold is the stone at the door. Threshold is the way in for independent Creators: an honest read on a project, what it needs most right now, and the next three moves, matched to real paths forward. Creators start free. Institutions license it for their programs.

Threshold connects the people, programs, studios and opportunities already serving Creators rather than replacing them. Technology supports the journey; Idu House surrounds it with people, services and real-world work. Capabilities shown reflect the intended product direction.

Where it stands

In build. Core flows are designed and the assessment questions and scoring rules are written. The private beta opens with film Creators first; music and original IP follow. During beta, a person on our team reviews every read before it goes out.

What compounds

Creative-specific workflow depth, permissioned first-party project and outcome data, institutional outcomes, repeated commercial and studio transactions, and relationships across Creators, employers and institutions. AI enables the system. It is not the moat by itself.
