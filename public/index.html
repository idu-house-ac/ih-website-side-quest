Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Access isn’t.

Plenty of creators have the ideas and the ability to make meaningful work. What they lack is affordable space, experienced collaborators, production resources, industry relationships or a clear path forward. Most creative systems only show up once someone has already proven themselves. Idu House is built to show up earlier.

Our work is rooted in Afrofuturism and diasporic storytelling, with a particular commitment to creators and communities that have been underfunded, overlooked or left out of traditional creative institutions, and it’s built for collaboration across cultures, industries and forms.

Talent is evenly distributed. Opportunity is not. So we build the rooms where they meet.

The name

IduPronounced EE-doo

Where it comes from

Idu is an older historical name for the Edo people and Edo land in present-day Nigeria. It doesn’t translate to “origin.” The Edo word closer to that is isi. But in Edo cosmology, the land once called Idu is understood as the origin of the world and the core of existence. That’s why we chose it. Idu House exists to be a place where things begin.

Rooted in Afrofuturism

Afrofuturism imagines futures built from African history, identity, culture and invention, with Black people and the diaspora as the future’s architects. It asks what the future could look like if we built from our own histories instead of leaving them behind. That question sits at the heart of the house.

The word house

A house is more than a building. It’s where people gather, trade knowledge, keep culture, challenge one another and imagine what comes next. We didn’t want another institution that felt inaccessible or run by gatekeepers. We wanted a home for possibility.

Rooted in where we come from. Built for what we can become.

Threshold

In development

Our creator platform, the thread through all of it. Threshold will help creators shape ideas into projects, see where they stand, find the right collaborators and opportunities, and build a record of the work they’ve done, with a path into Idu House services, productions and partners.

The long-term home is a physical creative campus with space, support, community and opportunity under one roof.

Where we’re going

From an emerging studio to a connected ecosystem.

Year 1

Build and prove

  • Launch the first version of Threshold with an initial creator community
  • Grow Brand Production and Creator Services
  • Deepen partnerships across creative, education, technology and industry
  • Develop our original IP toward production and distribution
  • Expand live programming and community events
Year 2

Expand and repeat

  • Bring Threshold to a larger creator community and more institutions
  • Make creator development and opportunity matching repeatable
  • Bring our first flagship original project to market
  • Grow our network of employers, brands, studios, educators and distribution partners
Year 3

Connect and scale

  • Connect creators, institutions, brands and studios through one ecosystem
  • Run multiple originals across development, production and distribution
  • Earn meaningful revenue from owned IP and creative partnerships
  • Open the first Idu House campus
